How it works

Invisible heating.
Beautiful warmth.

Gentle warmth rises from the baseboard and fills the room. No noise and no blowing air. Even warmth along every wall run, room by room.

How the warmth moves

Radiant plus
convective.

At its heart is a graphene heating element, the technology HeatENE is named for. No fans and no hot air blowing at you. Quick to respond, so rooms warm when you need them. See it on the thermal camera →

How the warmth moves

HeatENE warms the room two ways: radiant heat spreads out from the baseboard into the room, and warm air rises up the wall, gently circulates and returns low. The result is comfortable, even warmth along every wall run.

    How it's fitted

    Goes on
    like trim.

    Fit it yourself or use a contractor. The electrical connection must be made by a licensed electrician, or by you where your local code allows it, with a permit and inspection.

    1. 01

      Plan the runs

      Measure each room and note the doors; each run stops at the door casing. Runs shorter than 6 ft are cut to length on site at the marked cut lines, following the install guide. Runs of 2.5 ft or less use matching plain trim, the same profile but unheated.

    2. 02

      Fit the lengths and corners

      The baseboard goes on at the trim stage, with matching pieces at each corner. Runs finish straight into door casings at least 20 mm (3/4 in) deep.

    3. 03

      Connect and add thermostats

      Your electrician makes the connection, and each room gets its own thermostat. How it's installed →
